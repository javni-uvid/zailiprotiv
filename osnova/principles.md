# The principles of a javni-uvid project

The promises every project of the organization makes to its readers. Never changed without the maintainer. A
project's own AGENTS.md lists its principles whole, in its own words: it may promise more than this, and where
it differs on purpose it says so with the date of the maintainer's decision.

This file is a copy. It is written in `javni-uvid/osnova` (`shared/`) and brought here by the **osnova**
workflow: change it there, never here.

1. **The same rules for everyone a project counts.** No person, party, list or institution is hardcoded or
   singled out. Every measure is computed the same way for all, and lists are ordered by neutral rules (a count,
   the alphabet), never by preference. Where it can be tested, a test checks it.
2. **Facts from primary sources only:** the institution's own publications and the law as published. Reports by
   others (observers, the press, civil society) may help find or check something; they never supply a figure,
   change a ranking or make a finding. Where a project shows them, they are labelled as theirs, with the source,
   the date and a link.
3. **The site shows; it doesn't judge.** Something out of the ordinary is a question to check against the
   source, never a verdict. The site puts no label, tone or grade on a person, a party or an act, and says so.
4. **Only what a person does in public office.** Nothing about anyone's private life, and nothing of the site's
   own finding about a person. A private person is never named. Each project says whom it names and why; where
   it names no one, it links to the source's own document instead of copying a name from it.
5. **Nothing from third parties on the pages:** no cookies, no external fonts or scripts. An exception is the
   maintainer's decision, dated, listed in the project's AGENTS.md, and told to readers on the site itself.
6. **A source's words stay as they are.** A quote is word for word and checked against the source, never cut
   inside, softened or cleaned. A summary says that it is one, whose words it sums up, and who or what wrote it.
7. **Every figure can be followed to its source.** A page links to the document its figures came from, and the
   site explains how each measure is computed.
